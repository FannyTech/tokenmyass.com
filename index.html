FAQ

Questions people ask usLes questions qu'on nous pose

What is TokenMyAss?

A crypto token scanner. Give it a contract address, it analyses the on-chain data (liquidity, contract, holders, creator) and tells you whether the token looks legit or shows signs of a scam.

Which blockchains are supported?

Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Base, Polygon and Solana.

Is the verdict 100% reliable?

No, and no tool is. The verdict is an automated, indicative assessment based on on-chain signals. It doesn't replace your own research. A "Seems legit" token can still lose all its value.

Is this investment advice?

No. TokenMyAss doesn't tell you what to buy or when. It helps you spot technical risks. The decision, and the risk, stay yours. DYOR.

What about my privacy?

With private mode on, the app keeps no scans on your device. This website uses no cookies and does not track you: we only count anonymous page visits.

How can I reach you?

Write to us at contact@tokenmyass.com.
